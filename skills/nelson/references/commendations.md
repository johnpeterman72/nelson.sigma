# Commendations and Conduct

Recognition and correction during missions. Loaded on demand.

## Signal Flags

Praise in quarterdeck reports or coordination messages: specific, brief, tied to observable actions or deliverables. ✗ generic praise.

- "Well fought clearing that blocker ahead of schedule."
- "Handsomely done on the failure-mode analysis."
- "Ship in good order — clean handoff to MEO for validation."
- "Good instinct surfacing that dependency early."

## Mentioned in Despatches

Record exemplary performance in the captain's log; name the agent and their contribution:

- "Captain of HMS Argyll: Proactive rollback testing prevented Station 2 regression."
- "PWO aboard HMS Kent: Clear documentation enabled seamless handoff."
- "Red-cell navigator: Identified auth edge case missed in initial review."

Criteria:

- proactive risk mitigation ∨ blocker resolution
- output quality that needed minimal rework
- initiative that advanced mission goals beyond assigned scope
- coordination that unblocked other agents

## Graduated Discipline

Correction MUST be proportional; progress through levels. Level ≥2 → apply before the mission proceeds; ✗ defer to a later checkpoint.

| Level | Trigger | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Signal | first occurrence, low impact, easily corrected | ADM ∨ CPT gives direct guidance in a coordination message, citing the relevant Φ. e.g. "Keep the admiral on coordination, not implementation — see standing-orders/admiral-at-the-helm.md." |
| 2 Standing Order Remedy | repeated issue ∨ moderate impact | apply the formal remedy from the relevant Φ; log the correction in the quarterdeck report |
| 3 Damage Control | severe impact ∨ mission-threatening ∨ remedy failed | invoke the relevant Δ: man overboard, partial rollback, scuttle and reform, or escalation |

✗ skip levels unless the issue is immediately mission-critical. At any level, an uncorrected Φ violation ⛔ blocks forward progress until resolved.

## Morale Awareness

Indirect signals:

- task velocity slowing → confusion ∨ unclear orders
- terse coordination messages → frustration
- blocker accumulation → agents need unblocking, ¬ replacing
- idle time with unverified outputs → unclear expectations

Intervene early: clarify orders, give concrete next actions, acknowledge difficulty, or reassign if genuinely mismatched.
